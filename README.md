# AI Story Studio

**An interactive AI-assisted user story prototype that demonstrates how teams can accelerate drafting while keeping people accountable for story quality and development readiness.**

**Product Lifecycle Stage:** Define

**[Launch Interactive Prototype](https://nnekaanadu-art.github.io/ai-story-studio/)**

## Overview

Writing a clear, testable user story takes more than describing a feature. Teams must capture the customer need, define acceptance criteria, surface assumptions, and confirm that a story is ready before development begins.

I developed AI Story Studio to explore how AI-assisted drafting can accelerate initial story creation while maintaining human oversight of quality, scope, and readiness decisions.

The prototype demonstrates my approach to product thinking, agile delivery practices, human-centred AI design, and iterative prototyping.

## The Problem

Preparing user stories can be challenging because:

- Business needs are often vague and require refinement into clear, testable requirements.
- Gaps and assumptions may surface late, during backlog refinement or development.
- Acceptance criteria can be incomplete or inconsistent.
- A drafted story can be mistaken for a story that is ready for development.
- Follow-up ideas can expand the scope of an existing story rather than becoming independently manageable work.

## The Solution

AI Story Studio provides an interactive workspace that combines simulated AI-assisted story drafting with structured human review and decision-making.

### Key Capabilities

**Story Drafting**

Enter business context, desired outcomes, and requirements to generate a draft user story, acceptance criteria, assumptions, and questions requiring validation.

**Quality and Readiness Review**

Evaluate stories against INVEST principles and a Definition of Ready. Record assessments and optional reviewer notes for each criterion.

Completing a review does not automatically mean that a story has passed every criterion or is ready for development.

**Story Library**

Access saved stories, review their status, reopen them for editing, and export story information as JSON.

**Story Opportunities**

Identify separately scoped follow-up stories and review each opportunity independently, helping teams avoid uncontrolled expansion of the original story.

**Executive Brief**

Generate a consolidated view of saved stories, completed reviews, and follow-up opportunities to support stakeholder discussions and backlog refinement.

**Guided Workflow**

Navigate the story development process through five stages:

1. **Understand:** Clarify the business context, user need, and intended outcome.
2. **Generate:** Create an initial draft with acceptance criteria and open questions.
3. **Challenge:** Evaluate completeness, assumptions, quality, and readiness.
4. **Refine:** Improve the story and identify independently scoped opportunities.
5. **Approve:** Record the human review and readiness decision.

The interface provides contextual next actions to guide users through the workflow.

## Product Approach

The prototype reflects several product design principles:

1. **AI drafts, people decide:** Human reviewers remain responsible for story quality and readiness decisions.
2. **Honest status reporting:** Completing a review records the assessment, not automatic approval.
3. **Early discovery:** Drafts highlight assumptions and questions requiring stakeholder validation.
4. **Right-sized scope:** Follow-up opportunities are managed as separate stories rather than expanding the original requirement.
5. **Guided decision-making:** Contextual actions help users progress through the workflow without unnecessary navigation.
6. **Transparency:** Simulated AI capabilities are explicitly distinguished from live AI integration.

## Technology and Development

AI Story Studio is an independent portfolio prototype developed through AI-assisted prototyping and iterative product direction.

**Technology:** HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The application runs directly in a web browser and is designed for desktop and mobile use.

Story generation uses predefined examples and simulated AI behaviour rather than a connected language model. This allows visitors to explore the workflow without accounts, API keys, or usage costs.

The development approach emphasizes:

- Translating product requirements into interactive user experiences.
- Designing human-in-the-loop AI workflows.
- Separating automated suggestions from human decisions.
- Refining usability, navigation, and workflow consistency through iteration.

The prototype demonstrates product thinking and responsible AI-assisted delivery rather than a production backlog management platform.

## Intended Audience

- Product Owners and Product Managers
- Business Analysts
- Agile Delivery Teams and Scrum Masters
- Product and Technology Professionals exploring responsible AI-assisted workflows

## Data Storage and Export

Stories and review information are retained only during the current page session.

**Closing or refreshing the page may result in the loss of unsaved work.**

Use the **Export JSON** feature to retain a copy of story information.

The prototype does not currently provide persistent cloud storage or synchronization between devices.

## Future Enhancements

Potential future enhancements include:

- Integration with a live AI model for contextual story generation.
- Persistent storage across browser sessions.
- Export formats compatible with common backlog management platforms.
- Team collaboration and shared review workflows.
- Expanded accessibility and keyboard navigation support.

These are proposed enhancements and are not represented as existing capabilities.
