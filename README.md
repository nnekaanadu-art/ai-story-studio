# AI Story Studio

**An interactive user story prototype that shows how AI can draft user stories while people stay in control of quality and readiness decisions.**

## Overview

Writing a clear, testable user story takes more than describing a feature. Teams must capture the customer need, define acceptance criteria, surface assumptions, and confirm the story is ready before development begins.

I developed this prototype to explore how AI-assisted drafting can speed up the first version of a user story while keeping review and approval with people.

The project demonstrates my approach to product thinking, agile delivery practices, human-centred AI design, and AI-assisted prototyping.

## The Problem

Preparing user stories can be challenging because:

- Business needs are often vague and need to be shaped into clear, testable stories.
- Gaps and assumptions tend to surface late, during refinement or development.
- Acceptance criteria are sometimes incomplete or inconsistent.
- A drafted story can be mistaken for a story that is ready for development.
- Follow-up ideas can expand the scope of a story instead of becoming their own work.

## The Solution

An interactive workspace that combines AI-style story drafting with a structured human review process.

### Key Capabilities

**Story Drafting**

Enter a business context, desired outcome, and requirement to generate a draft user story with acceptance criteria and open questions to validate.

**Quality and Readiness Review**

Review each story against the INVEST principles and a Definition of Ready, with optional reviewer notes for each criterion.

**Story Library**

View saved stories and their review status, reopen them for editing, and export them as a JSON file.

**Story Opportunities**

Generate separately scoped follow-up stories and track which ones have completed a review.

**Executive Brief**

See a consolidated summary of saved stories, completed reviews, and the status of each follow-up opportunity.

**Guided Workflow**

Follow suggested next actions that move from drafting to review to summary, with a five-step approach: Understand, Generate, Challenge, Refine, and Approve.

## Product Approach

The prototype reflects several product design principles:

1. **AI drafts, people decide:** Readiness criteria are confirmed by a person, never automatically.
2. **Honest status:** Saving a review records that it was completed, not that every criterion passed.
3. **Early discovery:** Every draft includes assumptions and questions to validate with stakeholders.
4. **Right-sized scope:** Follow-up capabilities become their own stories instead of growing the original one.
5. **Clear next steps:** Each page guides users to the most useful next action.

## Technology and Development

This is an independent portfolio prototype developed through AI-assisted prototyping and iterative product direction.

The application uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and is designed to run directly in a web browser on desktop or mobile.

Story generation is simulated using predefined examples rather than a live AI model, so the prototype runs anywhere without setup or cost.

The emphasis is on demonstrating product thinking, workflow design, and responsible AI-assisted delivery rather than representing a production backlog management tool.

Potential future enhancements include:

- Improved accessibility and keyboard navigation.

## Disclaimer

AI Story Studio is an independent demonstration prototype created for educational and portfolio purposes.

Story drafts, quality suggestions, and opportunities are generated from simulated examples and are illustrative only.

Saved stories exist only while the page is open. Use Export JSON to keep a copy.

The prototype is not affiliated with any organization or intended to represent a production product management application.
